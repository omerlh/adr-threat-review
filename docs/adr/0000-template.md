# ADR NNNN: Short title

Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN

## Context
What forces are at play? What are we deciding?

## Decision
What we decided, in active voice.

## The four questions

### 1. What are we working on?
The component, the data it touches, and who or what can reach it.

### 2. What can go wrong?
Specific failure modes and abuse cases. "Nothing" is not an answer.

### 3. What are we going to do about it?
For each risk above: the mitigation, or the risk we accept and who owns it.

### 4. Did we do a good job?
The test, CI check or alert that tells us a mitigation stopped holding.

## Consequences
Positive, negative and neutral.
